Platform and Simulated Trading

Blastrr provides a simulated trading and evaluation platform. Orders placed in Blastrr accounts are simulated; they are not sent to an exchange or used to trade real market capital. Account balances, equity, buying power, and trading results are virtual figures, not cash deposits or withdrawable account balances. Account purchase fees, where applicable, are real payments and are separate from simulated balances.

Market data, platform tools, and educational materials are for general information only. They are not investment, financial, or trading advice, recommendations, or offers to buy or sell any financial instrument. Simulated execution may differ from live execution due to spreads, liquidity, slippage, latency, data delays, or provider outages. Simulated results do not predict or guarantee future performance.

Evaluation Rules, Account Limits, and Rewards

Each account is subject to its plan's currently published requirements, including profit targets, minimum activity, daily loss limits (DLL), and maximum loss limits (MLL). Loss limits apply as described in the plan rules and may include unrealized losses on open positions. Breaching a hard limit may trigger automatic trade closure, account locking, or account closure.

Any reward or performance payment is conditional on meeting the applicable plan rules and completing any required account review or verification. Displayed simulated profits alone do not guarantee qualification or payment. The current published plan and payout rules govern eligibility, calculations, and review.

Prohibited Trading Conduct

These rules apply to all Blastrr evaluation and simulated accounts. Attempts to evade the rules through multiple accounts, other people, or external platforms are also prohibited.

  • No hedging: Do not hold or coordinate opposing positions to offset or neutralize risk. This includes taking a long position in one account and a short position in another on the same instrument, or using closely related instruments or external accounts to create an offsetting position. Ordinary positions in unrelated instruments are not hedging just because they are open at the same time.
  • No news trading: Do not open, add to, reduce, or deliberately close a position, or place or modify an order intended to trade a scheduled high-impact economic release during the five minutes before or after it. This includes events such as CPI, major employment releases, and central-bank rate decisions that affect the instrument, as well as major scheduled asset-specific events. Remove pending orders that could be filled during the restricted period. Platform-triggered protective stops or account-risk closures outside your control are not by themselves a violation.
  • No micro-scalping or feed exploitation: Blastrr expects traders to follow a coherent, consistently applied trading method or system, with a general minimum holding period of two minutes. A trade held for less than two minutes is a review indicator, not an automatic breach; Blastrr assesses the full pattern and context. Repeated short-duration trades without a consistent method, or trades intended to capture isolated ticks, spread changes, stale quotes, price-feed delays, execution latency, or erroneous prices, may be treated as prohibited micro-scalping or feed exploitation.
  • No unauthorized automated trading: Bots, scripts, Expert Advisors, high-frequency systems, or external APIs may not generate or execute trades. The only permitted automated copying is Blastrr's built-in copy feature between accounts that you personally own and control.
  • No copying or coordinating with other traders: Do not copy, mirror, coordinate, or group-trade with another person's positions, signals, or accounts, whether manually, through a signal service, or through a third-party copy tool. Do not let another person trade or control your account, and do not use paid account-passing services.
  • No loss-limit or evaluation gaming: Do not use martingale or grid strategies, deliberate position-size escalation after losses, coordinated accounts, or other tactics to evade risk limits, manipulate evaluation results, or exploit account resets.
  • No platform abuse: Do not exploit software defects, provider errors, abnormal prices, or execution failures; interfere with platform services; or attempt to bypass account, order, or risk controls.

Blastrr may review trading and account activity. Where a violation is identified, actions may include cancelling affected orders, adjusting or removing non-compliant simulated results, denying reward eligibility, suspending or closing an account, or restricting platform access, subject to the applicable program rules.

General Risk and User Submissions

Live trading outside Blastrr involves substantial risk and may result in the loss of all capital committed. Blastrr's simulated results involve no live-market execution and should not be relied on as a record of live trading performance. You are responsible for ensuring that your use of the platform complies with laws applicable to you.

Testimonials, feedback, or metrics presented across Blastrr or third-party review channels reflect individual experiences in a simulated environment. They are not representative of all participants and are not promises or guarantees of results or rewards.

These platform terms supplement the applicable plan rules. If the rules or plan terms change, the version presented for acceptance governs your continued access and purchase eligibility.
