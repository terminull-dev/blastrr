Know the rules

Trade the plan.
Protect the downside.

Know your limits before the first position.

Daily loss limit — 5%

The daily loss limit is 5% of the plan's daily starting equity. A breach locks the account for 24 hours; when the lock expires, the daily starting equity resets to the then-current equity.

Maximum drawdown — 10% static

The maximum loss limit is 10% of the plan's initial simulated balance and does not trail profits. Breaching it permanently locks the account.

Profit target

Each plan has a stated target equal to 20% of simulated capital. Reaching a target does not by itself guarantee a payout or funded/live brokerage account; eligibility and review conditions apply.

News trading

News trading is prohibited under the stated rules. Review the complete policy for restricted events, time windows, open positions and enforcement.

Which markets can I trade?

The terminal lists Forex pairs such as EUR/USD, GBP/USD and USD/JPY, as well as supported crypto markets. The available instruments and market hours are shown in the trading terminal.

Is the capital real?

The plan capital is simulated. Any reward or withdrawal programme is subject to Blastrr's published terms, eligibility, verification where applicable, and payout rules.

Do I need KYC? Can everyone join?

Onboarding is wallet-first. Do not assume that wallet connection means no checks in every circumstance or that every jurisdiction is eligible. Check the current terms and applicable local restrictions before purchasing.

Straight answers

Questions before you launch?

Review the basics here, then check the complete rules in the app before purchasing a plan.

What does “straight to trading” mean?

You choose and purchase a plan rather than completing a multi-stage evaluation challenge first. Account activation remains subject to payment confirmation and the platform's terms.

Can I buy more than one account?

The stated model allows users to purchase multiple accounts within their budget. Check current account limits, prohibited behaviour and any aggregation rules in the app.

Which token can I use to pay?

USDT is supported on Ethereum, Base, Polygon, Avalanche, BNB Smart Chain, NullNet, and Solana. USDC is available only on networks with the configured official token: Ethereum, Base, Polygon, Avalanche, and Solana. The app filters options to match your connected wallet type.

How do withdrawals work?

Choose an available payout asset and network that matches your connected wallet type. The app records the destination network and token with your request for manual review and payment. Timing, minimum amounts, fees, eligibility and any reward split are governed by the current withdrawal policy.

When do Forex and crypto markets trade?

Forex typically trades around the clock on weekdays, with scheduled weekend and holiday closures. Crypto markets generally trade 24/7/365, although individual instruments, exchanges or the Blastrr platform may have maintenance or service interruptions. Continuous trading does not mean prices or liquidity are constant.

How does leverage work?

The terminal offers 1x mode and leverage options up to 50x on eligible instruments. Leverage magnifies exposure and can increase losses as well as gains; available limits depend on the instrument. All plan capital and trades are simulated.

How are discounts applied?

Account prices are shown at full price. Discounts appear only after you enter and apply an eligible code at checkout. Battle Pass discount certificates are single-use, wallet-bound, and may be restricted to a specific account type.
